Engineering Plastics Portfolio

We work with a comprehensive range of high-performance engineering plastics to meet the exact specifications of your industrial applications.

HDPE High-Density Polyethylene

Key Properties

  • Excellent impact resistance
  • High tensile strength
  • Low moisture absorption

Typical Applications

Chemical tanks, cutting boards, piping systems.

Nylon Polyamide (PA)

Key Properties

  • High mechanical strength
  • Good wear resistance
  • Low friction coefficient

Typical Applications

Gears, bearings, bushings, wear pads.

PP Polypropylene

Key Properties

  • High chemical resistance
  • Excellent fatigue resistance
  • Lightweight

Typical Applications

Packaging, automotive parts, living hinges.

Delrin Acetal / POM

Key Properties

  • High dimensional stability
  • Low friction
  • High abrasion resistance

Typical Applications

Precision gears, electrical insulator parts, rollers.

ABS Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene

Key Properties

  • Outstanding impact strength
  • Good machinability
  • Excellent aesthetic qualities

Typical Applications

Housings, prototypes, consumer goods.

PEEK Polyether Ether Ketone

Key Properties

  • Extreme temperature resistance
  • Exceptional chemical resistance
  • High mechanical strength

Typical Applications

Aerospace components, medical implants, pump parts.

PTFE Polytetrafluoroethylene

Key Properties

  • Unmatched chemical resistance
  • Extremely low friction
  • Wide temperature range

Typical Applications

Seals, gaskets, non-stick surfaces.

Teflon™ Premium PTFE Brand

Key Properties

  • Superior dielectric properties
  • Zero moisture absorption
  • High thermal stability

Typical Applications

Electrical insulation, chemical processing equipment.

UHMWPE Ultra-High Molecular Weight PE

Key Properties

  • Highest impact strength of any thermoplastic
  • Highly resistant to corrosive chemicals
  • Self-lubricating

Typical Applications

Chute linings, dock bumpers, wear strips.
