Versatile Sealing Solutions

Our solid rubber cords are manufactured using high-grade elastomeric compounds to ensure optimal performance across various environments. They are primarily used as custom O-rings, weather stripping, and gap fillers in machinery, automotive, and construction applications.

With excellent compression set resistance and elasticity, these cords maintain their shape and sealing properties even under continuous pressure, making them an indispensable component for leak prevention and vibration dampening.

  • Available in Nitrile (NBR), EPDM, Neoprene, and Silicone
  • Custom lengths and spool sizes available
  • Shore A hardness ranging from 40 to 90
  • FDA compliant grades available upon request

Key Advantages

Engineered for reliability, our rubber cords offer superior performance characteristics for demanding industrial applications.

High Durability

Excellent resistance to wear, tear, and abrasion, ensuring a long service life even in harsh industrial conditions.

Superior Flexibility

Maintains elasticity and flexibility across a wide range of applications without cracking or becoming brittle.

Temperature Resistance

Engineered to withstand extreme temperature variations, maintaining structural integrity in both hot and cold environments.

Cost-Effective

An economical solution for creating custom-sized O-rings and seals without expensive tooling or molding costs.
