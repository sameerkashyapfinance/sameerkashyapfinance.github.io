Industrial-Grade Materials

Selecting the right elastomer is critical for component longevity. We work with a comprehensive range of premium compounds.

EPDM

Key Properties

Excellent resistance to ozone, sunlight, and severe weather conditions. Outstanding heat and steam resistance.

Common Applications

  • Outdoor weather seals
  • HVAC components
  • Automotive cooling systems

Nitrile (NBR)

Key Properties

Superior resistance to petroleum-based oils, fuels, and hydraulic fluids. Good physical properties and abrasion resistance.

Common Applications

  • Oil seals and gaskets
  • Fuel hoses
  • Hydraulic pneumatics

Neoprene

Key Properties

Balanced combination of properties. Good resistance to moderate chemicals, oils, ozone, and temperature variations.

Common Applications

  • Vibration mounts
  • Industrial belts
  • General-purpose seals

Silicone

Key Properties

Exceptional high and low-temperature stability. Excellent flexibility, biocompatibility, and resistance to compression set.

Common Applications

  • High-temp gaskets
  • Pharmaceutical seals
  • Food-grade components

Natural Rubber

Key Properties

Unmatched flexibility, high tensile strength, and superior tear and abrasion resistance. Excellent dynamic properties.

Common Applications

  • Vibration dampeners
  • Shock mounts
  • Heavy-duty wear pads

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers to common queries about our rubber manufacturing capabilities.

What is the difference between rubber moulding and rubber machining?
Rubber moulding involves shaping raw, uncured rubber in a heated mould cavity under pressure (ideal for high volumes and complex shapes). Rubber machining involves cutting, turning, or milling pre-cured rubber blocks or cylinders to achieve extremely tight tolerances, typically used for prototypes or low-volume specialized parts.
Which rubber material is best for oil resistance?
Nitrile (NBR) is the industry standard for oil resistance. It provides excellent durability against petroleum-based oils, fuels, and hydraulic fluids, making it the preferred choice for automotive and industrial sealing applications.
Do you provide custom rubber components as per drawings?
Yes, Avenir Solutions specializes in custom manufacturing. We can produce bespoke rubber components based on your CAD drawings, 2D blueprints, or even reverse-engineer from existing physical samples to meet your exact specifications.
What industries use rubber components and seals?
Rubber components are critical across nearly all industrial sectors. Key industries include automotive, aerospace, chemical processing, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, heavy engineering, and packaging machinery, where sealing and vibration dampening are essential.
What are the typical lead times for rubber component orders?
Lead times vary based on complexity and volume. Standard catalogue items can often be dispatched within days. Custom moulded parts typically require 2-4 weeks for tooling and initial samples, followed by rapid production runs once approved.
Can you handle both high-volume and low-volume production?
Absolutely. We utilize flexible manufacturing processes. Our machining capabilities are perfect for low-volume runs and prototyping, while our multi-cavity injection and compression moulding lines efficiently handle high-volume, continuous production.
