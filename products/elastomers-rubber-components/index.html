Industrial-grade materials

Premium Elastomer Compounds

Selecting the right elastomer is critical for component longevity. We work with a comprehensive range of premium compounds.

EPDM

Key Properties

Excellent resistance to ozone, sunlight, and severe weather conditions. Outstanding heat and steam resistance.

Common Applications

  • Outdoor weather seals
  • HVAC components
  • Automotive cooling systems

Nitrile (NBR)

Key Properties

Superior resistance to petroleum-based oils, fuels, and hydraulic fluids. Good physical properties and abrasion resistance.

Common Applications

  • Oil seals and gaskets
  • Fuel hoses
  • Hydraulic pneumatics

Neoprene

Key Properties

Balanced combination of properties. Good resistance to moderate chemicals, oils, ozone, and temperature variations.

Common Applications

  • Vibration mounts
  • Industrial belts
  • General-purpose seals

Silicone

Key Properties

Exceptional high and low-temperature stability. Excellent flexibility, biocompatibility, and resistance to compression set.

Common Applications

  • High-temp gaskets
  • Pharmaceutical seals
  • Food-grade components

Natural Rubber

Key Properties

Unmatched flexibility, high tensile strength, and superior tear and abrasion resistance. Excellent dynamic properties.

Common Applications

  • Vibration dampeners
  • Shock mounts
  • Heavy-duty wear pads
