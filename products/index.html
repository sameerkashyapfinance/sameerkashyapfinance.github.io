01

Plastic Machining

Precision CNC machining of engineering plastics to tight tolerances for demanding industrial applications.

Materials

  • Nylon (PA6, PA66)
  • Delrin/Acetal (POM)
  • HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)
  • UHMW-PE (Ultra-High Molecular Weight PE)
  • ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene)
  • PPHP/PPCP (Polypropylene Homopolymer/Copolymer)

Typical Applications

  • Bushings and bearings
  • Rollers and pulleys
  • Gears and sprockets
  • Spacers and washers
  • Guides and wear strips
  • Wear pads and slides

Capability Highlights

  • CNC turning and milling
  • Tight tolerances (±0.001")
  • Complex geometries
  • Production from drawings
  • Material selection guidance
  • Quality inspection

Typical Applications

  • Sealing elements

Capability Highlights

  • Water jet cutting
  • Die cutting
  • Custom fabrication
  • Adhesive backing
  • Material selection
  • Prototype to production
