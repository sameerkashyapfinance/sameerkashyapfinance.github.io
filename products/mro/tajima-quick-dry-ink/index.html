The Standard in Construction Marking

In fast-paced concrete construction environments, waiting for ink to dry or losing critical layout lines to sudden rain can cause costly delays and errors. The Tajima Quick Dry Ink PSB2-180 is specifically formulated to bond instantly with porous and rough surfaces like concrete, cement blocks, and plaster. Drying in just 2 to 5 seconds, it leaves a high-density, weather-resistant mark that withstands heavy foot traffic, machinery, and harsh outdoor conditions, ensuring your reference points remain clear from layout to final inspection.

Key Use Cases on Site

Versatile application across all phases of structural development and civil engineering.

Layout Marking

Precise grid lines and boundary markings on concrete slabs before erecting walls or partitions.

Level Identification

Clear datum lines and elevation marks that remain visible throughout the construction phases.

Structural Reference Points

Marking anchor points, drill holes, and structural alignments on rough masonry surfaces.

Reinforcement Markings

Indicating rebar placement, spacing, and cutting points on cement blocks and foundations.

Excavation Indications

High-visibility markings for trenching, cutting, and core drilling on existing concrete.

Quality Checks & Workflow

Communicating inspection approvals, corrections, and workflow steps directly on the structural elements.
