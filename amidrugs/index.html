About Ami Drugs

Formerly Ami Bio Tech, Ami Drugs is the Ami Lifesciences company that runs the API plant at Adroda, Bavla. We bring the group's chemistry, regulatory depth and quality systems to every batch we make.

Learn more about us

Part of Ami Lifesciences

Ami Lifesciences is a Vadodara-based specialty API company serving customers in more than 60 countries. As its subsidiary, Ami Drugs shares the group's research, regulatory and quality infrastructure.
