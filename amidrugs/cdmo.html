How a project runs

From patent study to plant validation

Every development project moves through the same eight stages, so you always know where your molecule stands.

  1. 01

    Patent study

    Map the patent landscape so the chosen route is free to operate in your markets.

  2. 02

    Route of synthesis

    Select the most practical and cost-effective route to the molecule.

  3. 03

    Feasibility study

    Prove the chemistry at laboratory scale against the target quality.

  4. 04

    Process optimisation

    Improve yield, purity, cycle time and cost, step by step.

  5. 05

    Impurity control strategy

    Identify, limit and control impurities to regulatory expectations.

  6. 06

    Gram to kilogram validation

    Confirm the process holds as batch size grows.

  7. 07

    Scale-up validation

    Run scale-up batches with complete documentation.

  8. 08

    Plant validation

    Validate the process in the plant, ready for commercial supply.
