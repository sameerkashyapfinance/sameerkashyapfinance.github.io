Our teams

Where you could work at Adroda

Production

Run reactions, isolations and packing safely and to the batch record.

Quality assurance

Own documentation, change control, investigations and batch release.

Quality control

Test raw materials, in-process samples and finished API.

Engineering & utilities

Keep reactors, utilities and HVAC qualified and running.

Environment, health & safety

Protect people and the environment, from work permits to effluent.

Stores & supply chain

Receive, store and dispatch materials with full traceability.

How to apply

Send us your CV

Openings across the group are listed on the Ami Lifesciences careers portal. You can also email your CV with the role you are interested in.

View openings Email HR@amilifesciences.com

Beware of fake job offers

Ami Drugs and Ami Lifesciences never ask candidates for money at any stage of hiring, and we only send offers from our official email domains. If anyone asks you to pay for a job with us, do not pay, and please let us know.
