About Ami Drugs

An API manufacturer with a single focus, backed by the Ami Lifesciences group.

  1. Home
  2. About
Who we areOur groupValuesMilestonesLeadership

Ami Drugs at a glance

  • Company: Ami Drugs Pvt. Ltd., formerly Ami Bio Tech Pvt. Ltd.
  • Parent: Ami Lifesciences Pvt. Ltd., Vadodara
  • Manufacturing site: Adroda, Bavla, Ahmedabad district, Gujarat
  • What we make: Active pharmaceutical ingredients and intermediates
  • Group reach: Customers in more than 60 countries

Ami Drugs exists to do one thing well: manufacture active pharmaceutical ingredients to the group's quality standards, batch after batch, so customers get the attention of a dedicated site and the regulatory reach of a larger organisation.

Values

What we hold ourselves to

Draft values for management approval.

Quality first

No batch leaves the site unless we would trust it in our own family's medicine.

Data integrity

Complete, accurate and honest records, because every result is someone's evidence.

On time, in full

Customers plan production around our dispatch dates. We treat them as commitments.

Responsibility

Safe people, clean processes and careful use of solvents, water and energy.
