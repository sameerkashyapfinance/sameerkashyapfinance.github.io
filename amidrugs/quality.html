Quality & regulatory

Quality is not a checkpoint at the end of the line. It is how every step at Adroda is designed, run and recorded.

  1. Home
  2. Quality
Quality systemRegulatory supportCustomer auditsDocuments

Quality at Ami Drugs: at a glance

  • Standard: Current Good Manufacturing Practice for active pharmaceutical ingredients
  • Governance: An independent quality unit at the site, reporting into the Ami Lifesciences group quality function
  • Release: Every batch tested in the QC laboratory and released by quality with a certificate of analysis
  • Transparency: Customer audits and quality agreements welcomed
Quality management system

What are the core elements of our quality system?

Draft answers describing standard API GMP practice. The site QA head should confirm each one.
How is quality governed at the Adroda site?

A quality unit independent of production approves procedures, specifications and every batch release. Site quality reports into the group quality function, so standards, audit findings and improvements are shared across all Ami Lifesciences sites.

How are changes and deviations managed?

Every change to a process, material, equipment or document goes through formal change control with a documented impact assessment. Deviations are investigated to root cause, and corrective and preventive actions are tracked to closure and checked for effectiveness.

How is data integrity protected?

Laboratory and production records follow ALCOA+ principles: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate and complete. Computerised systems are validated, with access control and audit trails reviewed as part of batch release.

How are suppliers and materials controlled?

Key starting materials, solvents and packaging come only from qualified vendors. Incoming materials are sampled and tested against approved specifications before use, and critical suppliers are audited.

How are batches tested and released?

In-process controls check critical steps during manufacture. Finished API is tested against the agreed specification, and the complete batch record is reviewed by quality before a certificate of analysis is issued.

How are people trained and qualified?

Every operator, chemist and analyst is trained and assessed on the procedures they use before working independently, with periodic refresher training on GMP, safety and data integrity.

Regulatory support

Filing support from a team that files

The group's regulatory team maintains filings with authorities in the US, Europe, Korea, Japan and Brazil. Customers sourcing from Adroda can request letters of access, open-part documents and responses to agency queries for the relevant molecule.
