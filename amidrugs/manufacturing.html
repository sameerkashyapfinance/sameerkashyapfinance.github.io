Our Adroda manufacturing site: at a glance

  • Location: Survey No. 748/2, Adroda, Bavla, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
  • Reactor volume: 315 KL
  • Site area: 13,689 square metres
  • Products: Active pharmaceutical ingredients and intermediates
  • Licences and approvals: To be listed (state FDCA manufacturing licence, WHO-GMP, ISO)

The site runs under the Ami Lifesciences quality system, with processes developed and transferred by the group's research and technology centre.

Capabilities

From reaction to released powder

Equipment details to be confirmed by the site head.

Reaction

Glass-lined and stainless steel reactors for multi-step synthesis

Isolation & drying

Centrifuges, filters and dryers for solid isolation

Finishing

Controlled areas for milling, sifting, blending and packing

Utilities

Chilled brine, hot water, nitrogen, purified water and solvent recovery

Quality control laboratory

Tested before it travels

  • Chromatography: HPLC, GC, GC headspace
  • Spectroscopy: FT-IR, UV-Vis
  • Wet chemistry: Karl Fischer, titration, melting point, polarimetry
  • Stability & microbiology: ICH stability chambers, microbiology laboratory

Advanced techniques such as LC-MS, GC-MS, ICP-MS, XRD and particle size analysis are available within the group.
