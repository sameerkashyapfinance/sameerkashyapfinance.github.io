Material Selection Guide

Explore our comprehensive range of engineering materials with detailed properties, applications, and manufacturing capabilities.

Nylon (PA6, PA66)

Key Properties

  • High strength and toughness
  • Excellent wear resistance
  • Good chemical resistance
  • Low friction coefficient
  • Temperature range: -40°C to 120°C

Typical Applications

  • Gears and sprockets
  • Bearings and bushings
  • Rollers and pulleys
  • Wear pads and guides

Industries Served

Automotive, Food processing, Material handling, Packaging

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining
  • Injection Molding

Delrin/Acetal (POM)

Key Properties

  • Excellent dimensional stability
  • Low moisture absorption
  • High stiffness and hardness
  • Superior machinability
  • Temperature range: -40°C to 90°C

Typical Applications

  • Precision gears
  • Bushings and bearings
  • Valve components
  • Conveyor parts

Industries Served

Medical devices, Electronics, Automotive, Consumer products

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining
  • Injection Molding

HDPE

Key Properties

  • Excellent chemical resistance
  • High impact strength
  • Low moisture absorption
  • FDA compliant grades available
  • Temperature range: -50°C to 80°C

Typical Applications

  • Cutting boards
  • Chemical tanks
  • Wear strips
  • Food contact parts

Industries Served

Food processing, Chemical processing, Marine, Agriculture

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining

UHMW-PE

Key Properties

  • Exceptional wear resistance
  • Very low friction
  • High impact strength
  • Self-lubricating
  • Temperature range: -200°C to 80°C

Typical Applications

  • Wear strips and guides
  • Chute liners
  • Conveyor components
  • Sliding pads

Industries Served

Material handling, Mining, Food processing, Packaging

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining

ABS

Key Properties

  • Good impact resistance
  • Easy to machine and fabricate
  • Good dimensional stability
  • Excellent surface finish
  • Temperature range: -20°C to 80°C

Typical Applications

  • Housings and enclosures
  • Prototypes
  • Fixtures and jigs
  • Display components

Industries Served

Electronics, Automotive, Consumer products, Signage

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining
  • Injection Molding

PPHP/PPCP (Polypropylene)

Key Properties

  • Excellent chemical resistance
  • Low density and lightweight
  • Good fatigue resistance
  • FDA compliant grades
  • Temperature range: -10°C to 100°C

Typical Applications

  • Chemical tanks
  • Laboratory equipment
  • Food containers
  • Living hinges

Industries Served

Chemical processing, Food processing, Medical, Packaging

Manufacturing Services

  • CNC Machining
  • Injection Molding

Natural Rubber (NR)

Key Properties

  • Unmatched flexibility
  • High tensile strength
  • Superior tear and abrasion resistance
  • Excellent dynamic properties

Typical Applications

  • Vibration dampeners
  • Shock mounts
  • Heavy-duty wear pads

Industries Served

Heavy engineering, Automotive, Construction

Manufacturing Services

  • Compression Molding
  • Machining

EPDM

Key Properties

  • Excellent ozone and weather resistance
  • Outstanding heat and steam resistance
  • Good electrical insulation

Typical Applications

  • Outdoor weather seals
  • HVAC components
  • Automotive cooling systems

Industries Served

Automotive, HVAC, Construction

Manufacturing Services

  • Compression Molding
  • Extrusion

Nitrile (NBR)

Key Properties

  • Superior oil and fuel resistance
  • Good abrasion resistance
  • Good physical properties

Typical Applications

  • Oil seals and gaskets
  • Fuel hoses
  • Hydraulic pneumatics

Industries Served

Automotive, Oil & gas, Hydraulics

Manufacturing Services

  • Compression Molding
  • Machining

Neoprene (CR)

Key Properties

  • Balanced property profile
  • Moderate chemical and oil resistance
  • Good weathering resistance

Typical Applications

  • Vibration mounts
  • Industrial belts
  • General-purpose seals

Industries Served

Industrial equipment, Marine, Construction

Manufacturing Services

  • Compression Molding
  • Die Cutting

Silicone

Key Properties

  • Exceptional temperature stability
  • Excellent flexibility
  • Biocompatible, low compression set

Typical Applications

  • High-temp gaskets
  • Pharmaceutical seals
  • Food-grade components

Industries Served

Pharmaceutical, Food processing, Electronics

Manufacturing Services

  • Compression Molding

  • Outstanding chemical resistance
  • Excellent high-temperature performance
  • Low gas permeability

Typical Applications

  • Chemical seals
  • High-temp O-rings
  • Fuel system seals

Industries Served

Chemical processing, Aerospace, Oil & gas
